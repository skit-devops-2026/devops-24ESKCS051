General

Who can book a room or auditorium?

Any student or teacher at the college can submit a booking request. Requests are reviewed by the admin before a room is confirmed.

How long can I book a room for?

Bookings must be between 2 and 3 hours long. Shorter or longer requests will not be accepted by the form.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend submitting your request at least a day in advance so the admin has time to review and approve it before your event.

Approvals

What happens after I submit a request?

Your request appears in the admin panel with a "Pending" status. The admin checks whether the room is free at that time and either approves or rejects it.

How will I know if my request was approved?

You can check the status of your request on the "My Requests" table on the booking page. A notification system may be added in a later phase.

Can I cancel a booking after it's approved?

Not yet in this phase. Cancellation support is planned for a future update.
